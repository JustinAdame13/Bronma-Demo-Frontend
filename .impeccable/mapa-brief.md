# Mapa de clientes

Mode: Operate. Code-led. Primary scene: office supervision and prioritization, confirmed by the user. Scope: map route presentation only, including its navigation shell.

The user pinned a sober contemporary fintech direction and delegated composition. Preserve that commitment over the concept seed (seed:83e1702a068e5b30). Sources considered: financial statements, regional cartography, corporate identity manuals, route registers, credit dossiers, directory indexes, and executive report typography. Take their hierarchy and alignment, never imitate paper or tools.

## Direction contract

Charcoal navigation provides a stable frame for a light operational workspace. Gold marks selection; red, amber and green retain their existing account meanings. Local Helvetica Neue, tabular numerals and compact rows prioritize comparison over decoration.

First viewport: navigation on the left, compact page heading and import action, three prominent factual counts, then a single connected list/map work area. Route and search controls sit directly above the list they scope. No marketing hero or new invented data.

Signature interaction: selecting or hovering a client ties the list row to a charcoal emphasized map pin. The selected account opens its existing dossier without navigating away. Map controls use one visual vocabulary. On mobile the existing map/list switch preserves usable target sizes; on tablet the navigation becomes an icon rail.

Challenger assessment: the catalog/map duality reinforces list-to-location correspondence, but thumbnails would hide financial data; the graphite console contributes density and hairline discipline, but a fully dark map harms map legibility; airline paperwork contributes alignment, not stamps or sequential workflows; acetate tabs introduce unnecessary layered ornament. User-pinned product clarity and the existing BRONMA palette govern all translations.

Known risk: navigation is intentionally different only on the map to respect the strict scope. Other screens inherit no new styles. Sources, calculations, queries and event handlers remain intact.
